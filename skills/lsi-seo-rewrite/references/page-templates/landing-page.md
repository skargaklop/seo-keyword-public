# Landing Page Template

Template for creating conversion-optimized landing pages with LSI integration and human-sounding copy.

## H1 Formula

**Offer/Value Proposition + Primary Benefit + Urgency/Relevance**

### Examples

- "Free Shipping on Orders Over $50 - Get Your Package Faster"
- "Professional SEO Audit - Rank Higher in 30 Days or Less"
- "Exclusive Summer Collection - Limited Time Only Deals"

### Best Practices

- Keep H1 under 70 characters for optimal display
- Place primary keyword naturally within value proposition
- Include urgency or relevance signal (time-limited, exclusive, etc.)
- Make benefit immediate and specific

---

## Landing Page Structure

### Hero Section (Above Fold)

**Purpose:** Immediate value recognition and action.

**Structure:**

```markdown
## H1: [Value Proposition with Primary Keyword]

[Subheading: 2-3 sentences with secondary keyword, expanding on value and credibility]

[Primary CTA Button: Clear, action-oriented text]
- Examples: "Get Started Free," "Download Now," "Claim Your Offer"

[Optional: Trust signals below CTA]
- "No credit card required"
- "Trusted by 10,000+ customers"
- "30-day money-back guarantee"
```

**H1 Guidelines:**
- Include primary keyword naturally
- State the main benefit clearly
- Create urgency or relevance

**Subheading Guidelines:**
- 2-3 sentences maximum
- Include 1-2 LSI terms from commercial intent category
- Reinforce credibility or authority
- Support the H1 claim

---

### Problem/Agitation Section (100-150 words)

**Purpose:** Connect with user's struggle and create desire for solution.

**Structure:**

```markdown
### [Problem Statement H2 - includes pain-point LSI]

[Body paragraph addressing the specific struggle with target audience]

[2-3 sentences using pain-point LSI terms naturally]

[Transition to solution]
```

**Best Practices:**
- Write directly to ONE reader (SUCKS framework)
- Use specific language, not vague problems
- Integrate pain-point LSI terms naturally
- Avoid forbidden openers ("In today's fast-paced...")
- End with clear transition to solution

**Example LSI Terms (Pain-Point Category):**
- Struggle, difficulty, challenge, frustration
- Wasting time, losing money, missing opportunities
- Confusion, overwhelm, complexity

---

### Solution Section (150-200 words)

**Purpose:** Present your methodology and differentiation.

**Structure:**

```markdown
### [Your Approach/Methodology H2]

[Opening: Direct statement of your solution]

[Paragraph 1: Your methodology]
- What makes your approach different
- Solution-category LSI terms integrated

[Paragraph 2: Differentiation from alternatives]
- Why competitors fall short
- Comparison LSI terms

[Optional: Process breakdown in 3-5 steps]
```

**Best Practices:**
- Be specific about methodology
- Use concrete examples when possible
- Integrate solution-category LSI terms
- Avoid overclaiming without support
- Use FAB framework for key differentiators

**Example LSI Terms (Solution Category):**
- Methodology, approach, framework, system
- Strategy, technique, process, solution
- Proven, tested, validated, results

---

### Benefits/Features Section (FAB Format)

**Purpose:** Translate capabilities into user outcomes.

**Structure:**

```markdown
### Why Choose [Offer/Brand]

[Feature 1 - FAB structure]
**Feature:** [What it is with technical LSI]
**Attribute:** [Why it matters with metric/data]
**Benefit:** [User outcome with behavioral modifier]

[Feature 2 - FAB structure]
[Feature 3 - FAB structure]
[Continue as needed]

### What You'll Achieve

[Bullet list of outcomes]
- [Specific result 1 with timeframe]
- [Specific result 2 with metric]
- [Specific result 3 with improvement language]
```

**FAB Integration with LSI:**

- **Features:** Place technical/specification LSI terms
- **Attributes:** Include comparison LSI and metrics
- **Benefits:** Use behavioral outcome modifiers

**Example:**

```markdown
**Feature:** Our SEO framework integrates TF-IDF analysis from top-10 competitors
**Attribute:** This approach identifies high-value terms others miss, increasing topical coverage by 40%
**Benefit:** You'll rank for more long-tail queries and capture qualified traffic that converts
```

---

### Social Proof Section (if applicable)

**Purpose:** Build trust through credible evidence.

**Structure:**

```markdown
### Trusted by [Audience/Industry]

[Customer results with specifics]
- "Before: [state]. After: [specific result with numbers]."

[Testimonial quotes with attribution]
- "[Specific outcome quote]"
  — [Name], [Title/Company], [Timeframe]

[Trust signals with LSI]
- Awards, certifications, media mentions
- Customer count, success rate metric
- Industry recognition, expert endorsements
```

**Best Practices:**
- Use specific numbers and outcomes
- Include attribution with title/company
- Integrate trust-signal LSI terms
- Avoid vague "best service" claims
- Feature results similar to target reader's goals

**Example LSI Terms (Trust Signals):**
- Proven, verified, certified, accredited
- Industry-leading, recognized, endorsed
- Results, outcomes, achievements, success

---

### FAQ Section

**Purpose:** Handle objections and capture additional search traffic.

**Structure:**

```markdown
### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: [PAA-derived question 1]?**
A: [Direct answer with 2-3 sentences integrating LSI]

**Q: [PAA-derived question 2]?**
A: [Direct answer with 2-3 sentences integrating LSI]

**Q: [Objection-handling question]?**
A: [Address specific concern with reassurance]
```

**FAQ Sourcing:**
- Extract questions from Google PAA (People Also Ask)
- Include common sales objections
- Address timing, cost, complexity concerns
- Cover comparison with alternatives

**Best Practices:**
- Use actual questions from SERP PAA
- Answer directly without preamble
- Integrate LSI naturally in answers
- Keep answers concise (2-3 sentences)
- Address real objections, not softballs

---

### Final CTA Section

**Purpose:** Drive conversion with clear action.

**Structure:**

```markdown
### [Action-Oriented H2]

[Reinforce value in 2-3 sentences]

[Primary CTA Button]

[Optional: Secondary CTA link]
- "Learn more about [feature]"
- "View case studies"
- "Talk to sales"

[Closing trust reassurance]
- "No commitment required"
- "Cancel anytime"
- "See our guarantee policy"
```

**Best Practices:**
- Clear, single-action focus
- Remove friction from CTA
- Reiterate primary benefit
- Address remaining hesitation
- Avoid forbidden closers

## Landing Page LSI Focus Areas

### Commercial Intent Terms

Words indicating purchase or commitment readiness:

- Buy, purchase, order, invest, acquire
- Pricing, cost, rates, fees, plans
- Discount, offer, deal, promotion, savings
- Free trial, demo, consultation, quote

### Trust/Credibility Signals

Terms that establish authority and reliability:

- Certified, verified, accredited, licensed
- Proven, tested, validated, backed by
- Guarantee, warranty, protection, assurance
- Trusted, recommended, endorsed, approved

### Comparison vs Alternatives

Terms for competitive differentiation:

- Unlike others, compared to, versus
- Alternative to, instead of, better than
- Superior to, exceeds, outperforms
- Unique, exclusive, proprietary

### Urgency/Scarcity (Appropriate Use)

Time-sensitive language when genuine:

- Limited time, expires soon, deadline
- Exclusive, invitation-only, restricted
- Last chance, final opportunity
- While supplies last, capacity limited

**Caution:** Use urgency only when genuine and specific. Avoid false scarcity.

## Meta Tag Generation

### Title Tag

**Formula:** `[Value Proposition] | [Brand/Primary Keyword]`

**Requirements:**
- 50-60 characters maximum
- Include primary keyword
- Lead with benefit, not brand
- Create curiosity or urgency

**Examples:**
- "Free SEO Audit Tool | Identify Ranking Gaps Fast"
- "Professional Resume Writing | Land Interviews in 30 Days"

### Meta Description

**Formula:** `[Action verb] + [Benefit] + [LSI integration] + [CTA]`

**Requirements:**
- 150-160 characters maximum
- Start with action verb
- Include unique selling proposition
- Integrate 1-2 LSI terms
- End with clear CTA

**Examples:**
- "Transform your product pages into ranking assets. Our LSI framework integrates natural keywords that drive qualified traffic. Try free today."
- "Build resumes recruiters actually read. Our framework eliminates keyword stuffing while passing ATS filters. Get hired faster."

## Quality Checklist

### Structure & Formatting

- [ ] H1 follows value proposition + benefit + relevance formula
- [ ] Hero section includes subheading (2-3 sentences)
- [ ] Clear CTA in hero section with action-oriented text
- [ ] Problem/agitation section addresses specific struggle
- [ ] Solution section differentiates from alternatives
- [ ] Benefits/features use FAB format throughout
- [ ] FAQ section includes 3+ PAA-derived questions
- [ ] Final CTA section reinforces primary action
- [ ] Proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)

### Content Quality

- [ ] Hero subheading includes secondary keyword
- [ ] Problem section uses specific language (not vague)
- [ ] Solution section states methodology clearly
- [ ] Each feature follows FAB structure
- [ ] Social proof includes specific numbers/outcomes
- [ ] FAQ answers are direct without preamble
- [ ] Final CTA is single-action focused
- [ ] Total word count: 500+ words minimum

### LSI Integration

- [ ] Hero section includes primary keyword naturally
- [ ] Problem section includes 2+ pain-point LSI terms
- [ ] Solution section includes 2+ solution-category terms
- [ ] Benefits section includes technical LSI in features
- [ ] Social proof includes trust-signal LSI
- [ ] FAQ answers integrate LSI naturally
- [ ] 8-12 total LSI terms per 1000 words density
- [ ] LSI terms placed in priority: H1 > Lead > H2/H3 > Body > FAQ

### Anti-AI Pattern Compliance

- [ ] No correlative constructions ("X isn't just Y, it's Z")
- [ ] No forbidden openers ("In today's fast-paced...")
- [ ] No forbidden rhetorical flourishes ("The best part?...")
- [ ] No staccato phrasing abuse (max 1 per section)
- [ ] "Just" and "actually" used appropriately (not as fillers)
- [ ] No hedge words without genuine uncertainty
- [ ] Active voice used throughout (check passive)
- [ ] Conversational tone maintained (not formal)
- [ ] No corporate jargon without explanation

### Conversion Optimization

- [ ] Primary CTA is visually prominent and clear
- [ ] Value proposition stated in first screen
- [ ] Specific benefits (not vague promises)
- [ ] Objections addressed in FAQ or content
- [ ] Trust signals present where relevant
- [ ] Single action focus per CTA
- [ ] Urgency/scarcity used only when genuine
- [ ] Mobile-optimized formatting (short paragraphs)

### SEO Technical

- [ ] Title tag 50-60 characters with primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description 150-160 characters with CTA
- [ ] URL reflects primary offer (if applicable)
- [ ] Headings properly nested (H1 → H2 → H3)
- [ ] Internal links to relevant pages (if applicable)
- [ ] Image alt text descriptive with keywords
- [ ] Schema markup implemented (if applicable)
