   - Build author entities (`Person` + `sameAs` + author hubs).

4. **Authority canalization**
   - Concentrate internal authority flow to money pages.
5. **LLM content defense**
   - Raise content effort/originality with factual evidence and editorial standards.
6. **Managed reactivation**
   - Replace fake freshness with meaningful update cycles.
7. **Title semantic synchronization**
   - Align title/h1/url/intro to reduce mismatch and rewrite risk.
